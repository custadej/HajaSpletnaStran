What we do

Our core business is electrical engineering: from retrofitting and converting the controls of older lines to the complete electrical scope of new machines and robot cells.

01

Retrofit and conversion of existing line controls

Obsolete controls are the most common cause of unplanned downtime. We fit an existing line with a new controller, variable frequency drives and visualisation while keeping the mechanics that still serve well. The changeover is planned so that the production stop is as short as possible.

  • Replacement of obsolete PLC and HMI systems while the line keeps working
  • New control cabinets, schematics and documentation
  • Commissioning, testing and operator training

02

Electrical engineering for new machines and robot cells

For machine builders and end users we deliver the complete electrical scope: cabinet design, component selection, the controller program, HMI/SCADA visualisation and Fanuc robot programming. We also take care of safety functions and commissioning on site.

  • Control cabinet design and component selection
  • PLC, HMI and SCADA programming
  • Fanuc robot cells for machine tending

03

Support and performance optimisation

We stay available after commissioning. We fix faults, adapt programs to new products, extend the visualisation and shorten cycle times. Customers who have worked with us for many years are the best confirmation of this approach.

  • Diagnostics and fault clearing on the control system
  • Program and visualisation adjustments
  • Cycle optimisation and downtime reduction

How we work

  • We keep what works

    We do not replace for the sake of replacing. Mechanics and equipment that still serve well stay; we replace what threatens reliability.

  • Standard components

    We use equipment that maintenance staff know and that can still be bought ten years from now.

  • Documentation you can use

    Schematics, programs and manuals are handed over so the customer can maintain the system independently.

  • Availability

    We do not disappear after commissioning. Questions, additions and next phases are handled by the same team.

How a project runs

Clear steps, known responsibilities and no surprises at commissioning.

  1. 01

    Site visit and analysis

    We come to the plant, inspect the existing equipment and discuss the goals: what has to improve, what has to stay the same and how much downtime is available.

  2. 02

    Quotation and design

    We prepare a technical solution with component selection, electrical schematics and a schedule. The quotation is itemised so you know what you are paying for.

  3. 03

    Build and programming

    Control cabinets, the controller program, visualisation and the robot program are built in our workshop. Whatever can be tested before arriving at the plant is tested.

  4. 04

    Commissioning and training

    Installation, commissioning and optimisation on site. We train operators and maintenance staff on the new system and hand over the documentation.

  5. 05

    Support

    We stay available for questions, additions and further phases. Most customers work with us for years.
